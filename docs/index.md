  - title: Nothing leaves your Mac
    details: No account, no telemetry, no network service. The interface talks to an agent bound to loopback, started with the app and stopped with it.
  - title: Honest about what it finds
    details: Removed, skipped and failed are reported separately, sizes are measured rather than estimated, and a partial scan says so.
